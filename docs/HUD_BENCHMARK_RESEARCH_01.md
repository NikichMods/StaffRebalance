# StaffRebalance — HUD Benchmark Research 01

**Date:** 2026-11-09. **Status:** external research and UX recommendation; no UI implementation, no user approval of final layout, no HUD runtime PASS. Branch `dev/reserve-core-01`.

**Project state:** Astral/Fire staff and melee relevant game gates PASS; Ice/Thunder have static+mock/CI PASS but **runtime NOT TESTED**, deliberately **DEFERRED** by the owner until the elements become available. Do not block independent HUD/visual work or subsequent balance on their availability, but do not claim a four-element game PASS. See [STATUS](STATUS.md), [REQUIREMENTS](REQUIREMENTS.md), [HUD Design 01](HUD_DESIGN_01.md).

## Why research was necessary

Player must infer four facts: **(1)** how many empowered staff actions remain, **(2)** each actually performed strong staff action costs one unit independent of hit, **(3)** unique melee hits restore units, rest at Conduit refills, and **(4)** zero means *weak unlimited staff shots*, **not unable to fire**. This resource is separate from AP/APR, AstralPool, the 0/50 diagnostic tally and elemental selection.

The original proposed three diamonds are only a counter. They do not communicate the earning rule or the weak-at-zero behavior. Avoid calling an unconstrained, context-free decorative indicator self-explanatory.

## Game analogues and what they teach

| Game | Verified mechanism / HUD evidence | Transfer and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **Hyper Light Drifter** | The closest behavioral analogue. Alex Preston described the sword/gun ammo loop as a design evolved from playtesting: striking enemies or objects refills projectile ammunition. In a later interview he says players repeatedly **forgot this rule even after lingering instructional text**. [NYU Game Center, 2017](https://gamecenter.nyu.edu/2017-igf-interviews-hyper-light-drifter/); [Game Developer, 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-i-hyper-light-drifter-i-balances-difficulty-with-fairness). | Merely showing charges / a one-time prompt does not ensure causal comprehension. Reward genuine melee credit with a conspicuous but brief local refill cue. **Do not copy its environmental-object recharge:** StaffRebalance expressly excludes scene objects and destructibles. |
| **Hollow Knight** | Nail hits earn SOUL; SOUL is spent on spells/healing. At a spell-ready threshold the meter briefly flashes with a soft sound. [Community Hollow Knight Wiki — Soul](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Soul). | Use an affordance of **readiness** and short feedback when a usable strong shot becomes available; keep state visible between actions. A continuously filling vessel suits fractional currency, whereas StaffRebalance's current indivisible actions favor explicit units. |
| **Lies of P** | Combat hits build segmented Fable Slots for weapon arts; the blue segments are adjacent to the HP/stamina group, and ability costs are shown near the ability affordances. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/lies-of-p-tips/); [Fable Arts guide](https://samurai-gamers.com/lies-of-p/fable-arts-guide/). | Existing HUD resource adjacency + discrete segments reduce decoding cost. However a player can still neglect the ability despite its visibility; pair count with purpose/feedback. Our one unit per strong shot is simpler than Fable Arts varying slot costs. |
| **Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown** | Successful hits build Athra and special moves consume it. Ubisoft describes gameplay control reminders and tutorials and accessibility adjustment for Athra charge/depletion; the design team describes vivid effects signifying successful powerful moves. [Ubisoft accessibility spotlight](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/game/prince-of-persia/news-updates/5nGZiBSFtcEzFd93QlTotS/prince-of-persia-the-lost-crown-accessibility-spotlight); [Ubisoft gameplay description](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/e1SD5gR3TWqk5GPAjWHSz); [interview, Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/01/17/prince-of-persia-the-lost-crown-builds-on-raymans-platforming-legacy/). | Feedback must reflect the importance of an event: small replenishments get small animations, full reserve/rest may get a slightly stronger one. Avoid theatrical super-ability effects for every ordinary staff shot. |
| **Remnant II** (secondary) | Weapon Mod Power accumulates on damage; a portion can come from melee, and rest at World Stone restores Mod Power. [Community wiki — Mod Power](https://remnant2.wiki.gg/wiki/Mod_power). | Confirms value of an earn/spend/rest triangle across genres, but its continuous damage-based economy is **not** the StaffRebalance contact-based +1 contract. |

**Not an empirical universal winner:** peer-reviewed HUD research reports that composition/spatial organization and user expertise/genre can affect experience; a 2026 tablet-shooter study saw no significant *main effect* for numeric/icon/bar HUD type in its particular test. These contexts do not establish that gems, a bar, or numbers always win. [2015/16 empirical study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1071581915001779); [2026 study abstract](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390027276689291520).

## Designer principles with primary professional sources

1. **System-status visibility and prompt feedback** — current availability and the effect of a just-performed action should be clear. [Nielsen Norman Group — visibility of system status](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visibility-system-status/).
2. **Recognition, not memory tests** — offer in-context hints and visible cues instead of relying on a one-time tooltip or instructions shown outside combat. [NN/g — recognition and recall](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/).
3. **False affordances are expensive** — a drained charge display must never imply that staff firing itself is disabled, as it remains available but weak. Do not use lock, prohibition slash, “0 ammo” or a greying-out of the staff control as the primary state cue. [Celia Hodent, The Gamer's Brain, GDC16](https://celiahodent.com/gamers-brain-ux-onboarding/).
4. **Learn by doing** — timely, minimal prompts placed after first relevant spend and first actual melee refill are more useful than an isolated long tutorial. In this project do not assume asset/localization/one-time-persistence behavior until tested. [Celia Hodent, GDC16](https://celiahodent.com/gamers-brain-ux-onboarding/).
5. **Sight plus optional other senses** — illuminated vs empty units must remain distinct by shape/fill/outline, not hue alone. Use sufficient contrast on both bright/dark game backgrounds; don't make audio the only cue. [Microsoft Xbox Accessibility Guideline 101](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101); [XAG 102](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102); [IGDA Game Accessibility SIG](https://igda-gasig.org/get-involved/sig-initiatives/resources-for-game-developers/sig-guidelines/). These are **design goals for accessible defaults**, not a claim that this mod currently exposes size/contrast settings.
6. **Test the user's actual interpretation** — do not ask only “looks good?”. Observe whether an unbriefed player figures out the source, sink and behavior at zero, then test a later recall/recognition moment. Small qualitative sessions find usability misunderstandings; this is a proposed project acceptance test, not statistical proof. [NN/g, qualitative user counts](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-many-test-users/).

## Derived proposal for StaffRebalance (not adopted final visual)

**Primary recommendation: a small staff-associated segmented *empowerment* indicator as a native-HUD sibling.** Three filled/outlined units at current dev `capacity=3`, grouped with the lower-left native health/AP status row but visually distinguished from AP and ordinary ammo. Anchor the resource to staff semantics with a staff-head/rune glyph or a short first-exposure label, not just unlabeled diamonds. Same resource regardless of Astral/Fire/Ice/Thunder. The 3-segment concept is a temporary UI expression, not a locked final cap; support current/capacity dynamically and consider an alternate compact layout if balancing later raises capacity substantially.

**Cause and effect must be legible:**

| Game event (authoritative) | Recommended UI response | Explicit negative condition |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed strong staff action, including a miss | Immediately extinguish exactly one filled segment, optionally a brief inward/consumption motion. | Never consume at raw button press or on secondary projectile hits. |
| Weak staff action at reserve zero | Show unchanged empty units **with a small alive/dim staff glyph**; optional one brief “weakened” cue at the transition to zero, not a repeated error on every shot. | Never gray out/hide the staff ability or draw a crossed-out gun. Unlimited weak shots remain legal. |
| Unique melee credit with capacity available | Illuminate exactly the newly added segment; short outward “charge gained” pulse located *at the meter*. | Duplicate source-target and contact with destructible/object must not give a false gain cue. |
| Eligible melee contact while already full | No false +1 or fill animation. Optionally brief non-incrementing acknowledgement only if later testing shows an interpretation problem. | No visual overflow/counter beyond gameplay capacity. |
| Successful Conduit rest | Fill missing units to capacity and play one modest “fully replenished” cue. | Opening/closing rest menu or transport does not fill. |
| Fade/menu | Inherit native menu hiding and coordinate own dim/temporary emphasis with CombatHUD 0.4.1. | Do not write into CombatHUD's 17 existing targets, change HUD_Panel visibility, or create always-on top-level overlay. |

**Layout candidates to compare visually, no final selection:**

- **A: left-bottom HP/AP neighbor (recommended first prototype)** — status grouping fits the accepted CombatHUD/UMG hierarchy, while a staff/rune glyph identifies the *independent* resource. Watch for confusion with AP; render as a separate compact block, not part of the AP bar.
- **B: near the staff weapon/action icon** — strong semantic proximity to the spender, but must remain noticeable when wielding melee, otherwise the player does not see the recharge they just earned. Requires live layout binding evidence and compatible keyboard/gamepad positioning before selecting.
- **C: by crosshair / central character** — conspicuous during combat, but repeated updates and camera action risk visual clutter; do not make it the default without player tests.

**Event feedback proposed timing for test, not sourced hard standard:** fast state transition in the same game-thread cycle in which gameplay reserve changes; micro pulse approximately 0.20–0.40 s and optional temporary visibility lift around 0.6–1.0 s. Animations must be interruptible and converge on authoritative current value, never drive state. Avoid every-melee HUD spam; cap/duplicate must not fabricate gains. Keep numerical value and empty-state distinction readable with animations disabled.

**Onboarding concept:** at the first meaningful *loss* show a localized one-time-in-session, short contextual explanation near the staff resource; at the first genuine *gain* show a short “melee hit replenishes” explanation or animated link. No block of text every fight. Localization strategy and exact copy remain a UX decision; never hard-code controller physical keys or assert a persistent tutorial flag without its owner/semantics. Consider starting with the always-visible resource motif and an event animation alone, then add a sentence only if unbriefed testing finds it insufficient.

## Host technical constraints: use proven sibling facts without overclaiming

[CombatHUD 0.4.1 source](https://github.com/NikichMods/CombatHUD/blob/main/Scripts/main.lua) uses in-viewport `HUDMainUMG_BP_C`, native `LeftBottom_Panel` Canvas and separately controls **17 child opacity targets**. It also preserves the native common HUD/menu owner. [Asterigos-Research R-005](https://github.com/NikichMods/Asterigos-Research/blob/main/docs/VERIFIED_RUNTIME.md#r-005--native-hud-menu-owner-и-границы-opacity-групп) confirms the shared menu boundary. [R-015](https://github.com/NikichMods/Asterigos-Research/blob/main/docs/VERIFIED_RUNTIME.md#r-015--projected-screen-components-vs-viewport-hud-root-2026-10-29) has an independent custom Canvas runtime show/remove PASS in the existing viewport root; it does **not** by itself prove StaffRebalance's proposed precise sibling position or coexistence/fade/cleanup.

StaffRebalance should own its new widgets and read **only the authoritative `session.reserve`**; no second mirrored currency in settings or other mods. One bounded GameThread UI lifecycle/update worker, no `LoopAsync`, no broad `ProcessEvent` hooks; reconcile on actual changes, disappearance/recreation of HUD/owning Hilda, menu and mod shutdown. Do not alter frozen CombatHUD 0.4.1. Runtime test with CombatHUD both enabled and absent. Initial UI prototyping may use authored simple primitives without redistributing game assets or fonts. Any additional host UI feasibility blocker needs a focused proof, not a new large research framework.

## Proposed qualitative acceptance tests

Without explaining the mechanical loop first, ask a novice player to play a few minutes with Astral/Fire. Observe whether they can infer: (a) why a unit disappears even after a miss; (b) why a successful melee contact lights it; (c) that empty resources mean weaker fire, not no fire; (d) why hitting an object fails to refill; (e) that Conduit rest restores all; (f) the same reserve carries between weapons/elements. Then check comprehension after a distraction. Also perform UI-native acceptance: icon distinct from AP, readable on dark/light scenes at target resolution, 0.2 idle under CombatHUD, reveal on meaningful events, menu/cutscene native hiding, no overlap with quick items, correct cleanup and no duplicate widgets after rebind. Tests on unavailable Ice/Thunder are **not** preconditions to begin this work.

## Research verdict

**Use the original three-segment concept as a baseline, not the completed design.** Highest expected benefit comes from semantic identification (this is staff *strength*, not ammunition), direct visual cause/effect on spend and melee recharge, and accurate weak-at-zero signaling. Implement only after a small comparative visual study (A vs B) or one targeted accepted layout choice, then mock and live-test on available Astral/Fire. Production remains BLOCKED. Neither this research nor an attractive static icon proves self-explanatory UX without observing real players.

## Follow-up: readable shot silhouette (2026-11-09)

The owner suggested a **diegetic companion cue** to the reserve indicator: keep vanilla strong staff projectile visual size/brightness, and modestly reduce weak staff projectile size/glow. This addresses the game's perceived oversized/brilliant magic FX and makes strong-versus-weak legible even with the HUD faded. Visual identity must follow the executed shot/child effects, **not current currency after debit**. Research and third-party-mod limits: [STAFF_VFX_FEEDBACK_RESEARCH_01](STAFF_VFX_FEEDBACK_RESEARCH_01.md). No implementation or game VFX PASS. Interface still needs to convey exact reserve count, earning rule and that empty reserve means weakened rather than disabled staff use.
